How to help

  1. Press Start listening and allow the microphone.
  2. Tap Adhan, then recite the full adhan. Tap Iqama before the iqama.
  3. Try different distances and volumes, with a pause of ~10 s in between.
  4. Press Download recording and Download results and send both files to us.
Works best in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari on a laptop or phone. The model (~17 MB) downloads once. Audio is processed on your device and only leaves it if you download and send it yourself. On a phone, keep the screen on and this page open while testing.
